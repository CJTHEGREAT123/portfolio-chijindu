About Me

Engineering Meets Innovation

I study Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering at Afe Babalola University, and I taught myself software for the same reason I like aircraft: both reward people who understand the whole system. Right now that means writing interfaces in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, working in C++, Python and Node.js, and modelling parts in AutoCAD and Autodesk Inventor, on demo builds where I can take the whole thing apart and put it back together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can you build right now?

Multi-page websites in hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus scripting and logic in C++, Python and Node.js. The demo builds on this page are the honest evidence of that: practice projects, not client deployments.

Have you worked with clients?

Not yet. Everything here is either an ALCHIVON build or a project I made to learn. I would rather say that plainly than dress up a demo as a delivery.

What is the CAD work for?

I study Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering at ABUAD, so AutoCAD and Autodesk Inventor sit alongside the code, doing 2D drafting and 3D part modelling for mechanical and aerospace concepts.

Do you design as well as build?

Yes. I taught myself graphic design in CorelDRAW and Photoshop before I got deep into code, and I use Figma to lay an interface out before writing any of it.

What are you learning next?

Game development and 3D animation: Unreal Engine with C++, and Blender beyond basic modelling. They are listed separately from my skills for a reason: I am learning them, not claiming them.

What do you do at ALCHIVON?

I am a Co-Founder, and I hold the Co-CEO and CFO roles: company strategy and direction, financial planning and budgeting, operations, and working with the team on the product and technical roadmap.
